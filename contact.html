Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Can I use my own photo, logo, or artwork? +

Absolutely. You can send your own design, or choose from a design offered by Lacci Studio. Every order is handled with care and close attention to detail.

How does pricing work? +

Everything in the shop has its price listed, and personalization is included. Bulk, business, and fully custom projects are quoted — send the details with the form above.

How do I pay? +

Shop orders are paid by card at checkout. For quoted custom orders we’ll send you payment details with your quote.

Will I see my design before it’s made? +

Yes, if you want to. Choose “send me a proof” when you personalize your item and we’ll email a digital proof to approve before we print.

Can I return a personalized item? +

Because every piece is made for you, we can’t accept returns or exchanges. If something arrives damaged or doesn’t match the proof you approved, email info@laccistudio.com with a photo.

What if I’m not sure what I want? +

That’s completely fine — send us your idea and we’ll help you figure out the best product and approach.
