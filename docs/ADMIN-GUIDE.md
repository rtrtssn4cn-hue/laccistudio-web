# Lacci Studio Admin — owner guide

Written for the owner. Covers only what the new admin can do today; it grows with each release.

**Where:** LacciStudio.com/admin/studio/ (works on iPhone and computer). The classic editor at LacciStudio.com/admin/ stays available.

**Logging in:** the first time on a device, tap **Log in** — it opens the classic editor's GitHub login. After logging in, come back to /admin/studio/ and tap **I've logged in**.

## How changes go live

Every change goes through three steps, so nothing reaches customers by accident:

1. **Change** something. The bar at the bottom shows "unsaved changes". Your edits are kept on this device even if you close the page.
2. **Save draft.** Saved, but customers still see the old version. The bar turns yellow: "saved changes not live yet".
3. **Publish.** The live website updates in about a minute. Or **Discard** to throw the draft away.

## Products screen

Each product shows **Available** and **Featured** switches.

- **Available ON** = shown in the Shop and can be bought.
- **Available OFF** = hidden: not shown and can't be bought, even from an old link. Nothing is deleted; switch it back on to restore it.
- **Featured ON** = can appear in **Featured Gifts** on the homepage (up to 3, and only if Available is on).

Filters: All · Active · Hidden · Draft · Featured · Seasonal · Archived, plus Search and a category list.
Several at once: tick them (or **Select all**) → **Hide · Unhide · Feature · Unfeature · Archive**.

## Product workspace

Tap a product to open it. The top shows its status, category and price with the two switches; the tabs are:

- **Overview** — name, description, category, subcategory. *Advanced*: Draft, Seasonal or Archived (all off sale).
- **Pricing** — base price and every option price in one table, with what the customer pays (and per item for coaster sets). A changed price shows "was $…" until published. Switched-off choices fold under **Inactive**. **Price planning** holds Balanced and Premium prices for later; they are never charged until you tap **Use Balanced/Premium prices** and publish.
- **Media** — **+ Add images**, drag (or arrows on a phone) to reorder, ★ Primary, Replace, remove; add or remove a video. *Advanced*: the blank product photo used by the customizer.
- **Variants** — colours (Available list, Hidden list folded; search, select, show/hide selected), sizes and other options as on/off switches, and a colour × size overview.
- **Personalization** — upload, text, designs, blank, preview, proof and notes switches and the fonts offered. Used by the new customizer.
- **Shipping** — packed weight in grams (checkout adds up the weight to pick the shipping price).
- **SEO** — search title and description, saved for when products get their own pages.

The card on the right (or **Show preview** on a phone) shows how the product looks in the Shop as you edit.

## Rules the server enforces

- Products, option choices and colours are never deleted — they are switched off or archived.
- A product that is Available must keep at least one choice of each option, and one colour, switched on.
- New pictures are saved into the draft and go live when you publish.

## Undo

- Right after a change: tap **Undo** on the message at the bottom.
- Before saving: **Undo all** in the bottom bar.
- After saving but before publishing: **Discard**.
