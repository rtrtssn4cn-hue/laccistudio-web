# Lacci Studio — Fees, Competitor Pricing and Shipping Research

Research date: 2026-09-26. Read-only web research.

## Read this first: gaps in the data

- **Etsy blocks automated reading.** Every etsy.com and help.etsy.com page returned HTTP 403 or a CAPTCHA, and CAPTCHAs were not bypassed. Etsy fee figures below come from search-result text quoted from etsy.com / help.etsy.com pages, meaning Etsy's own wording as indexed. The pages themselves could not be opened, so "last updated" dates could not be read.
- **No Etsy competitor listing prices could be verified.** Search results gave Etsy listing titles and URLs but no prices. Part B uses independent shops (mostly handmade or small-batch), Personalization Mall / Things Remembered, Uncommon Goods, Shutterfly, Walmart Photo and CVS Photo instead. Amazon product pages returned 500/503 errors, so there is no Amazon Handmade price data.
- **Treat all Part B ranges as indicative.** Most categories have 3–7 data points, not a full Etsy sample. Before setting final prices, check the Etsy search pages in a normal browser.

Typical Etsy take on a $30.00 order with shipping included and no Offsite Ad: 6.5% ($1.95) + 3% + $0.25 ($1.15) + listing fee $0.20 = **$3.30, or 11.0%**. If an Offsite Ad gets the sale, add 15% ($4.50), for **$7.80, or 26%**.

#### Etsy and lower prices on your own website

- **No price-parity rule found.** Etsy's Seller Handbook says pricing is at the seller's discretion. Neither the Seller Policy nor the Fees policy (as they appear in search results) has a rule stopping you from charging less on your own site. Status: no parity clause found in search results; the full Seller Policy text could not be opened. **UNVERIFIED as a negative.**
- **What is prohibited is steering Etsy buyers off Etsy.** Etsy's Fees policy and its "Off-Platform Transactions" page (etsy.com/legal/policy/off-platform-transactions/1254654515806, from search results) treat fee avoidance as strictly prohibited. That includes encouraging buyers to buy an Etsy item somewhere else, pointing them to another venue by QR code, or finishing an Etsy-started sale off Etsy. **So do not put website discount codes or QR codes in Etsy orders or messages.** Your own site can still be cheaper for customers who reach it directly.
- A separate legal point, not an Etsy rule: a "compare at" or original price must be a genuine price that was actually charged for a substantial period (FTC deceptive-pricing guidance).

### Snipcart (own website cart)

- **2% of sales**. If monthly sales are **under $1,000**, a **$20/month minimum** applies instead. Payment gateway fees (Stripe or PayPal) are charged on top. Source: https://snipcart.com/pricing (read 2026-09-26; no date shown on the page).
- What this means: at $400/month in sales, the $20 minimum equals 5% of sales, not 2%. The 2% rate only applies once sales reach $1,000/month.

### Stripe (standard US)

- **2.9% + $0.30** per successful domestic card charge. International cards add 1.5%; currency conversion adds 1%. No monthly fee. Source: https://stripe.com/pricing (read 2026-09-26).

### PayPal (standard US commercial)

- **PayPal Checkout / Pay with Venmo: 3.49% + $0.49**
- **Standard credit/debit card payments: 2.99% + $0.49**
- International transactions add 1.50%.
- Source: https://www.paypal.com/us/business/paypal-business-fees (page states last updated **1 Sep 2026**).

Same $30.00 order on your own site through Snipcart + Stripe, at $1,000+/month volume: 2% ($0.60) + 2.9% + $0.30 ($1.17) = **$1.77, or 5.9%**. Through PayPal Checkout instead of Stripe: $0.60 + $1.54 = **$2.14, or 7.1%**.

### Key July 2026 changes

- **Commercial ounce tiers were removed.** Every package under 1 lb now costs the 15.99 oz rate, so a 4 oz coaster pays the same as a 15 oz mug box. Zone 8 at 4 oz went from about $6.36 to $8.40 (reported by a secondary source).
- Packages over 1 cubic foot now use a dimensional divisor of 139 (was 166). All dimensions round up to the next whole inch.
- Under-1-lb packages to rural ZIP codes, AK, HI, PR and military addresses are charged extra.

### Discount levels

- **Pirate Ship:** says it offers rates *below* Commercial Pricing (Commercial Plus / USPS Connect eCommerce level) for the lower 48 states, with automatic cubic rate-shopping, $100 insurance included, and "up to 86%" savings vs retail (its own marketing claim). Exact per-zone Pirate Ship rates were not visible on the page. **UNVERIFIED** beyond "at or below Commercial."
- **Etsy Labels:** secondary sources say Etsy label buyers pay Commercial rates. Etsy's own page could not be opened. **UNVERIFIED**: assume Commercial (the table above) as the working number.

### Houston shipping rule of thumb (approximate)

For a 1–2 lb mug or tumbler box, budget **$8.00–$8.50 in zones 1–4** (most of Texas and nearby states), about **$9–10 in zone 5**, and **$11–13 in zone 8** (West Coast, Northeast). Coaster sets under 1 lb cost $6.93–$8.40.
